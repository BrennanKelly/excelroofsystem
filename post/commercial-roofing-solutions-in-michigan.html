Our Commercial Roofing Services

Roof Installations

Complete commercial roof installations tailored to your building's specific requirements. We use the best materials and proven techniques to ensure your new roof provides lasting protection and energy efficiency.

Commercial Roof Coatings

We specialize in commercial roof coatings, flat roof waterproofing, and industrial roof replacement to ensure your property remains secure in any weather. Our coatings are designed to extend the life of your roof while improving energy performance.

Maintenance & Inspections

Regular maintenance is key to extending the life of your commercial roof. We recommend having your commercial roof inspected at least twice a year—once in the spring and once in the fall. Additionally, inspections should be conducted after severe weather events to identify and address any potential damage.

Emergency Roofing Repairs

When disaster strikes, our team provides emergency roofing repairs to address urgent issues and prevent further damage to your property. We understand that a compromised roof can't wait, and we respond quickly to protect your business.

Weather Protection for Michigan Businesses

Michigan's harsh winters demand roofing systems that can handle the elements. We use specialized commercial roof coatings and reinforced materials to protect roofs against heavy snow loads and extreme cold temperatures. Our solutions are specifically designed for the challenges of the Michigan climate.

Safety First

Excel Roof Systems follows strict safety protocols, including employee training, on-site safety measures, and the use of cutting-edge equipment to minimize risks. The safety of our team and your property is always our top priority.
